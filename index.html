        Security reminder

        Nobody from IT will ask for your password

        CUNY and campus IT never ask for your password or MFA codes by phone, text, or email. Don't approve an MFA prompt you didn't start.

        Password rules
        • At least 8 characters
        • An uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, and a number or special character
        • Can't contain your username
        • Can't change again within 5 days; can't reuse your last 4
